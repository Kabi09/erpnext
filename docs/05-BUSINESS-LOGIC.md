# Business logic and source evidence

The rows below were checked by reading the named local source. They capture selected operation boundaries, dependencies and side effects. The [source index](../metadata/source-index.json) has broader automated candidates with file paths and line numbers.

| Area | Observed upstream behavior | Required target service |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Material request | Issue and transfer update ordered quantity by original row and reject quantities above the stock quantity plus configured allowance. Manufacture uses a separate quantity check. Updates roll up percentage and linked Work Order counters. [Source](../reference/erpnext/erpnext/stock/doctype/material_request/material_request.py#L481) | Material request service with requested, issued and remaining quantity in stock UOM. Default to a strict cap as requested. Make any tolerance a separately approved compatibility mode |
| Stock entry | Submit dispatches purpose-specific behavior, updates reservations, posts the stock ledger and GL, requests reposting and updates Material Request quantities. Cancel runs linked-state checks and refreshes reservations and request totals. [Source](../reference/erpnext/erpnext/stock/doctype/stock_entry/stock_entry.py#L488) | Stock posting and reversal service with availability by dimension, immutable ledger lines and reservation rules |
| Purchase receipt | Submit checks approving authority and updates preceding documents and billing state before stock and GL. It also updates reservation and production-plan receipt quantities. [Source](../reference/erpnext/erpnext/stock/doctype/purchase_receipt/purchase_receipt.py#L374) | Receipt and returns service sharing valuation, serial and batch allocation, status rollups and posting |
| BOM and Work Order | BOM has explicit cost recalculation. Work Order submit refreshes demand and planned quantities, creates Job Cards and optionally reserves stock. Cancel validates first and releases or recomputes related state. [BOM cost entry point](../reference/erpnext/erpnext/manufacturing/doctype/bom/bom.py#L1279) · [Work Order](../reference/erpnext/erpnext/manufacturing/doctype/work_order/work_order.py#L690) | Versioned BOM, cycle checks, UOM-aware explosion and costing, Job Card operations, WIP, consumption and finished goods |
| Sales invoice | Submit checks approval and preceding status, updates billing, posts GL and may also post stock when configured. Returns, assets, credit limit and timesheets have additional branches. [Source](../reference/erpnext/erpnext/accounts/doctype/sales_invoice/sales_invoice.py#L447) | Billing and posting service with invoice/fulfillment line links, decimal taxes, returns, closed periods and credit policy |
| Payment entry | Submit requires zero difference amount, performs withholding and schedule work, posts the GL and refreshes outstanding amounts, dunning and status. [Source](../reference/erpnext/erpnext/accounts/doctype/payment_entry/payment_entry.py#L267) | Payment allocation and reconciliation with explicit exchange rates, validated outstanding and audited reversal |

For partial issuance, gate each operation on a submitted eligible request, matching item/line, convertible unit, positive issue quantity and expected parent version. Inside the transaction, reject any delta greater than remaining quantity or permitted stock, update the counter and ledger once, roll up header state and return remaining quantity and version. Cancellation posts inverse events and restores derived counters. Concurrency tests must prove two valid-looking requests cannot overshoot. Downstream returns, backdating, revaluation, FIFO, moving average, standard costing and landed cost require separate rule reviews. [Stock ledger](../reference/erpnext/erpnext/stock/stock_ledger.py) · [GL](../reference/erpnext/erpnext/accounts/general_ledger.py)

Continue manual reviews by dependency cluster. Sales and CRM need lead conversion, pricing and order fulfillment. Buying needs supplier controls, purchase order tolerances and invoice matching. Subcontracting needs outward supply and inward receipt, ownership and consumption. Assets need capitalization, depreciation and disposal. Projects, Quality, Support, Maintenance, Regional, Telephony, EDI, Portal and framework modules need their own rules and fixtures. Identify exact controller, caller, hooks, settings and upstream test examples for each port. Test examples are source evidence until executed against the target.
